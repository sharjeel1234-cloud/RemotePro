5 Best LED Desk Lamps to Protect Your Eyes While Working

Raat ko der tak kaam karne se aankhon mein dard (eye strain) ho sakta hai. Ek achi desk lamp sirf roshni nahi deti, balkay aapki focus ko barhati hai aur neend ki quality ko kharab nahi hone deti.

1. The Premium Bar: BenQ ScreenBar

Pros: Monitor ke upar lagti hai, screen glare zero hai, auto-dimming feature.

Cons: Bohat mehangi hai.

2. Best Budget: TaoTronics LED Desk Lamp

Is mein 5 color modes aur 7 brightness levels hain, jo har tarah ke kaam ke liye fit hain.

Lamp Lete Waqt "Color Temperature" Check Karein

Warm White: Raat ke waqt relax karne ke liye.

Cool Blue: Subah ke waqt focus barhane ke liye.
