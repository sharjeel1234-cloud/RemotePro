7 Best Ergonomic Chairs for Small Home Offices in 2026

Agar aap ghar se kaam karte hain aur sara din purani kursi par guzarte hain, to peeth ka dard (back pain) hona aam baat hai. Lekin masla tab hota hai jab aapke paas bari chair rakhne ki jagah na ho. Is article mein hum ne un behtreen ergonomic chairs ka intekhab kiya hai jo na sirf aapki back ko support karti hain balkay chotay kamron (small apartments) ke liye bhi perfect hain.

1. The Mesh Master: Sihoo M18

Ye chair un logon ke liye best hai jo sasti aur mazboot cheez dhoond rahe hain. Iska mesh design garmiyon mein aapki back ko paseenay se bachata hai. Pros Adjustable lumbar support, Breathable.
Cons Thori bari feel ho sakti hai.

Thori bari feel ho sakti hai.

2. Space Saver: Herman Miller Sayl

Agar aapka budget thora zyada hai, to ye dunya ki sab se iconic chair hai. Iska design "Golden Gate Bridge" se inspire hai aur ye bohot kam jagah gerti hai. Pros: Outstanding design, 12-year warranty. Cons: Mehangi hai.

3. Best for Budget: IKEA Markus

IKEA ki ye chair sadiyon se remote workers ki pasand rahi hai. Iski height zyada hai jo lambay logon ke liye behtreen hai. Pros: Simple setup, High back support. Cons: Armrests adjustable nahi hain.

Kursi Khareedte Waqt Kya Dekhna Chahiye? (Buying Guide)

Lumbar Support Kia ye aapki kamar ke nichlay hisse ko support karti hai?
Adjustability Kia aap iski height aur armrests ko set kar sakte hain?
Material: Mesh (hawa dar) behtar hai ya Leather?

Conclusion Aik achi kursi sirf aik kharcha nahi balkay aapki sehat mein aik investment hai.
Agar aapka apartment chota hai to Herman Miller Sayl ya Sihoo M18 behtreen options hain.
