5 Best Noise-Canceling Headphones Under $100 for Remote Work (2026)

Ghar se kaam karte waqt bacchon ka shor, kitchen ki awazein, ya bahar traffic ka noise aapki concentration kharab kar sakta hai. Agar aapka budget $100 se kam hai aur aap premium quality noise cancellation chahte hain, to ye list aapke liye hai.
Hum ne in headphones ko unki battery life, comfort, aur noise block karne ki salahiyat par test kiya hai.

The Value King: Anker Soundcore Life Q30

Anker ki ye series dunya bhar mein mashhoor hai. Is mein "Hybrid Active Noise Cancelling" hai jo 95% low-frequency sounds ko block kar sakti hai.

2. Most Comfortable: Sony WH-CH720N

Sony ke premium headphones mehnge hote hain, lekin ye model unhi ka sasta version hai. Ye bohot light-weight hain, jis se aap 8-10 ghante lagatar pehn sakte hain. Pros: Super light, Great sound quality, Dual connection (2 devices). Cons: Plastic body thori sasti feel hoti hai.

Best for Travel: SoundPEATS Space

Agar aap saste mein "Deep Noise Cancellation" chahte hain, to ye ek naya aur behtreen option hai. Inki battery 120 hours tak chalti hai. Pros: Amazing battery, Foldable design. Cons: Mic quality average hai.

Headphones Khareedte Waqt 3 Zaruri Cheezain (Quick Tips)

ANC (Active Noise Cancellation): Check karein ke noise cancel karne ka button hai ya nahi. Battery Life: Kam az kam 30 ghante ki battery honi chahiye. Comfort: Earpads naram (soft foam) hone chahiye taake kaan mein dard na ho.

(Conclusion) Agar aapko best noise cancellation chahiye to Anker Q30 lein, aur agar aap light-weight headphones pasand karte hain to Sony CH720N behtreen hain.
